# Lucerna's complete theme records

These are source design records, not reduced token recipes. LAMPLIGHT records the retained 0.2.0 appearance; APERTURE and MARGIN were authored for the 0.3.0 explorer. The choices are installed, not yet owner-approved individually. The actual interface is the preview; no concept image is claimed as executable UI.

## Revised skin applications — 2026-09-26

The owner rejected the first APERTURE and MARGIN applications as insufficiently
distinct. The full source theme blocks below remain intact; these applications
supersede their earlier shared-layout embodiment. A theme is the broader sensory
identity. Its skin is the particular interface body built from that identity.

APERTURE now separates a vertical optical navigation dock from the working field.
A fixed refractive bitmap sits behind suspended, asymmetrically edged source
panes; an inset metadata gutter frames the leading source. The reader separates
its outline, instrument and source/notebook glass. In narrower views the optical
dock becomes horizontal; source search and Questions remain available. The real
scientific diagram keeps its semantic colors and controls.

MARGIN now opens a bound light research folio. An annotation margin flanks a
single ruled source register, with oxide marks, paper edges and a different
typographic cadence. Source reading and notebook belong to the folio; the actual
scientific stage remains dark and framed. Narrow views stack the page without
replacing its useful actions. LAMPLIGHT is retained without redesign.

Actual library and open-reader captures received main-author inspection and a
fresh independent desktop visual review. Browser-local switching and narrow
reflow were checked; owner aesthetic approval remains open. Complete skins and
their bitmap/font assets travel into self-contained HTML exports. No concept art
is represented as an application screenshot.

## THEME — LAMPLIGHT

🎨 NAME: LAMPLIGHT
🌈 COLOR PALETTE:
Base: #0b1118 — dark instrument cabinet.
Primary: #e9c796 — warm learning signal and action.
Highlight: #8fd8ed — retained cyan instrument signal; paper is #e9e5da.
Text: #ece9e1 — warm legible reading against the cabinet.
🧠 MOOD: A quiet desk with a working scientific instrument; warmth without ornamental spectacle.
❤️ EMOTION: A difficult subject feels approachable and worth staying with.
🔤 FONT PREFERENCE: Existing system sans controls and serif source/display hierarchy; retain the installed baseline fallbacks.
🔍 VISUAL MOTIF: A source at the desk, a mechanism in the instrument, a tether connecting them.
💬 VIBE TAGS: lamplit study, source-linked instrument, warm precision, patient inquiry.
🌀 ANIMATED EFFECTS: Only the authored teaching scene; no decorative theme motion.
📦 USE CASE: Sustained evening study and the original Lucerna workflow.

Identity and lineage: Retained baseline application, not a new theme claim. The warm signal belongs to thinking and useful action, not decorative chrome. Keep source/experiment distinctions, reading hierarchy and existing semantic diagram colors. Adapt only the selector placement needed to offer choices. Assets: packaged style.css and the original scientific scenes; no added artwork or remote font.

## THEME — APERTURE

🎨 NAME: APERTURE
🌈 COLOR PALETTE:
Base: #080b14 — the space behind a suspended optical plane.
Primary: #b8e9ff — a clear, cool opening into the idea.
Highlight: #d6f5ff — fine luminous inner seam, sparingly used.
Text: #e8f2fa — readable light, not a glowing halo.
🧠 MOOD: Calm modern optics: separate panes float in a low-light research room.
❤️ EMOTION: Curiosity with room to breathe; complexity becomes inspectable.
🔤 FONT PREFERENCE: Segoe UI Variable / Segoe UI humanist sans, light large display headings, ordinary-weight controls and source text.
🔍 VISUAL MOTIF: A lens opens into a source; suspended panes hold distinct kinds of thought. Curves, inner-edge light and separated layers reinforce that relationship.
💬 VIBE TAGS: suspended optics, luminous seams, rounded instruments, cool clarity, breathable research.
🌀 ANIMATED EFFECTS: Authored mechanism motion only. Pane depth is static; reduced-motion needs do not lose the identity.
📦 USE CASE: Exploratory technical learning, source collections, an open mechanism beside its supporting passage.

Identity: Distinct surfaces make a difficult idea available through an aperture. Organising form: separate curved masthead, floating library panes, rounded instrument and source-tether vessels. Material: low-opacity blue-black planes, restrained inset seam, soft exterior shadow. Type: broad humanist headings avoid the folio's bookish mass. Controls: soft capsules, rounded sequence chips, cool active state. Background: quiet radial depth, not an illustrated dashboard.

Keep: Separation, curved perimeter, restrained optical edge light and a clear hierarchy. Adapt: Pane sizes and grid according to real text and available screen width. Real constraints: source accuracy, scene math, meaningful data colors, route/learner state, narration and controls must work. Inherited habits that may change: masthead attachment, square card housings, heading font and control silhouette. No fake gauges, chapter numbers, scientific visuals or status data.

## THEME — MARGIN

🎨 NAME: MARGIN
🌈 COLOR PALETTE:
Base: #eeeee7 — pale archival stock, not a white tech shell.
Primary: #813c2f — oxide marginal mark connecting an idea to its source.
Highlight: #fafaf4 — the open paper's reading plane.
Text: #252e30 — dark editorial ink on light stock.
🧠 MOOD: A rigorous research folio, with a real dark scientific instrument sitting on the page.
❤️ EMOTION: Orientation, ownership and the satisfaction of keeping what clicks.
🔤 FONT PREFERENCE: Georgia serif source/display hierarchy; system sans controls and metadata, preserving mathematical typography.
🔍 VISUAL MOTIF: The margin is where sources acquire connections. Bibliographic gutters, oxide rails and double rules organise a source rather than enclose every item in the same box.
💬 VIBE TAGS: research folio, editorial ink, bibliographic gutter, oxide margin, dark instrument on paper.
🌀 ANIMATED EFFECTS: Scientific scene motion only. Page stock and rules remain still.
📦 USE CASE: Source reading, argument reconstruction, notebook work, daytime study and mixed paper/experiment sessions.

Identity: Understanding is written beside evidence, not over it. Organising form: a single-column source ledger with a bibliographic gutter, straight double-rule masthead and reader seam, paper source panes, framed dark experimental stage. Material: opaque stock and a small offset paper shadow; no glass glow. Type: serif hierarchy separates it from APERTURE. Controls: straight, inked and restrained, with a clearly legible active scene. Background: continuous quiet stock.

Keep: The gutter, reading hierarchy, marginal rail and distinction between paper and instrument. Adapt: Collapse the gutter on narrow screens; scientific stages stay dark to preserve their supplied color semantics. Real constraints: readable equations, source provenance, scene controls and learner state. Mutable habits: dark whole-page background, generic boxed library grid and rounded controls.

## Application and evidence

Target: The complete actual Lucerna explorer, including Library, Experiment, Read, Connections, search, questions and source dialogs. Theme selection changes only the root appearance and its browser-local preference; it does not author or reload an explanation. Exports inline these same assets.

Option relationships: LAMPLIGHT retains a warm instrument cabinet; APERTURE suspends cool optical planes; MARGIN uses a light bibliographic folio and dark instruments. Shared navigation and data meaning are functional constraints, not a mandate that all housings look alike.

Visual commitments: The geometry, material, typography and library organisation described above must all be present in actual-content states. Approval: no separate concept reference or individual owner approval claimed. Main-author rendered review inspected real source/experiment and library states; complete review evidence is kept in the source repository's development/theme-options-0.3.0.md and the Scrapbook collection. These records cannot replace that observation or establish screen-reader conformance.
