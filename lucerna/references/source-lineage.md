The derivative references turn that expertise into the actual host/runtime workflow. They preserve the supplied sources' useful judgment while changing their role from standalone persona prompts into integrated teaching responsibilities. The host keeps its own identity. Whole canonical prompts remain available for deeper source-specific design work.

Omnicompetence governed capability construction; EGDOD informed state ownership, interface boundaries, revisions and recovery; Arcweft informed the source/scene workspace and accessible interaction; Promptcraft governed the model-facing operational text. The resulting package carries the runtime craft and code it uses. Those design skills are not runtime installation dependencies.

Research informed the design without supplying efficacy claims for this package. Relevant primary studies include [Kestin et al. on a structured AI tutoring intervention](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6), [Bastani et al. on assisted performance and later learning](https://hamsabastani.github.io/education_llm.pdf), [Mayer and Chandler on learner-paced narrated animation](https://tecfa.unige.ch/tecfa/teaching/methodo/Mayer_Chandler01.pdf), and [Roediger and Karpicke on retrieval and delayed retention](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x). Their interventions and measured outcomes have their own boundaries. None establishes that any generic prompt, generated animation or this new Augment produces the same outcome.

The working design adapts representations, support, opportunities for retrieval and route structure to the learner's actual goal and conversation. It carries no fixed learning-style diagnosis, universal video-length limit, compulsory quiz sequence or claimed learning-rate multiplier. Its exact runtime guarantees concern the implemented operations: source preservation, validated scene publication, local state, curriculum/pathway management and re-entry.

## Version 0.2.0 implementation inputs

The memory work draws an engineering lesson from Zhou et al., *Filesystem-Based Memory for LLM Agents: Organization, Evolution, and Sustainability*, arXiv:2607.26637v1: retained facts can lose temporal, contextual and relational meaning. Lucerna's typed-memory changes are an original adaptation to this runtime, not a result that the paper tested. Its memory lab identifies the reported benchmarks and distinguishes synthetic illustrations and hypothetical cost reuse from observations. No full paper is included in the customer package.

KaTeX 0.18.7 is included locally for mathematical typesetting under its MIT license; its license is retained alongside the runtime. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md. The original supplied educator prompts remain unchanged.
