# Dynamic chemical equilibrium

This original teaching example models a well-mixed reversible first-order reaction A to B and B to A. The forward reaction rate is kf[A] and the reverse reaction rate is kr[B]. Total concentration [A]+[B] is conserved.

## The mechanism

Starting with concentration one of A and zero of B, the B fraction at time t is kf/(kf+kr) times (1-exp(-(kf+kr)t)). At equilibrium the forward and reverse rates are equal and generally nonzero. The equilibrium B fraction is kf/(kf+kr). Increasing both rate constants by the same factor preserves equilibrium composition and changes the rate of approach.

## Model boundaries

This is an illustrative single-step kinetic model, not a claim about the elementary mechanism of every chemical reaction. Temperature, total concentration, mixing and volume stay fixed. Concentrations use arbitrary consistent units.
